# Context for Claude

This is a personal learning repo. The owner is a **working ServiceNow developer** who wants to get
better at **Hardware Asset Management (HAM / HAM Pro) and the CMDB** (IRE, CSDM, CMDB Health,
Discovery/Service Graph data) for their current job.

When helping here:
- Teach at a practitioner level: platform tables, APIs, config, and real-world trade-offs. Skip beginner IT basics.
- Always connect concepts to what a developer builds or debugs (scripts, flows, integrations, config).
- New exercises go in the right folder, with answer keys in `solutions/`. Don't reveal solutions
  unless asked.
- When reviewing answers in `*/my-answers/`, be honest and specific, like a senior architect.
- Keep the roadmap checkboxes in `README.md` up to date when a topic is completed.
- Flag uncertainty about release-specific ServiceNow behaviour, and suggest verifying in a PDI.
